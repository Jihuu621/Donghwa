# Dubu look mechanics

Dubu is a compact stylized humanoid MC with a large expressive head, swept black hair, a beige zip jacket, charcoal trousers, and a stable planted stance. His lower body stays anchored on one baseline; do not rotate or tilt the whole sprite.

His gaze is led by both eyes moving naturally within their existing eye construction, then by a subtle head-and-neck turn and a restrained upper-torso follow-through. Eyelids and brows reinforce vertical attention: raised eyes and slightly lifted chin for up; lowered eyelids, chin, and gaze for down. The nose and face plane turn toward screen-right or screen-left while preserving his facial proportions. Hair, jacket, hands, and trousers stay structurally stable, with only very subtle follow-through.

Cardinal families: 000 looks upward with frontal body and lifted eyes/chin; 090 turns head and face clearly toward the viewer's screen-right with the rightward face plane and eye direction; 180 looks downward with frontal body and lowered eyes/chin; 270 is the exact opposing head/face turn to the viewer's screen-left. Diagonals progress evenly between these families in 22.5-degree steps. No new prop, shadows, detached effects, labels, or replacement eyes.
